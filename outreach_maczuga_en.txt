Subject: A dimension-uniform a posteriori certificate for SPDEs — math.NA endorsement

Hi Paweł,

I'm Hubert Lipski, working independently on certified spectral methods for
(S)PDEs. Attached is a short paper that extends the RVPINN line (Rojas,
Pardo, Paszyński et al.) — the discrete dual-norm residual as an a
posteriori certificate — to a linear bandlimited SPDE on T^d.

The core result: in the mode-space representation (Whittle–Matérn basis in
space, half-period basis in time) the certificate decomposes per mode, so
its constant is uniform in dimension d — verified empirically from d=2 to
d=7 (J from 14 to 6217 modes). Separately, I show the natural Dirichlet
time basis produces a false error plateau (not a discretisation artifact,
but a loss of self-adjointness), which the certificate flags and raw
convergence does not.

The paper survived an independent from-scratch reproduction (fresh code,
no access to the original scripts), which caught a bug in one metric (a
missing square root in evaluation) — the correction is documented directly
in the text, and the repository is fully reproducible.

I don't yet have an arXiv endorsement for math.NA. Would you be willing to
consider sponsoring the submission? Happy to answer anything about the
proof, the certificate construction, or the experiments — and if this
isn't your area, I'd appreciate a pointer to whoever in the group might be
a better fit.
